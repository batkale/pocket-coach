What this tool can't tell you
  • The play call is unknown, so a flagged error may be the protection design, not the player.
  • Only dropback pass plays are covered. There is no run blocking.
  • Tracking ends about 0.5 s after the throw, so nothing after the catch is visible.
  • Eight weeks (Weeks 1-8, 2021) means small samples. Check the opportunities column and the 90% interval before acting.
  • "Likely" flags are rules, not PFF grades. Check the film.
  • Cost is the whole play's EPA shared across its flags, not the cost of the error itself.
