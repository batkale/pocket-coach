What this tool can't tell you

  • The play call is unknown, so a flag may reflect the protection design rather than the player.
  • Only dropback pass plays are covered. There is no run blocking.
  • Tracking ends about 0.5 s after the throw.
  • Eight weeks of data means small samples. Always read the sample size and the interval.
  • "Likely" flags come from a rule, not a PFF grade. Check the film.
  • Cost is the whole play's EPA shared across its flags, not the cost of the error itself.
